## The margin that matters

Fixed costs: $0 a month (Ferramentas (estimativa, gratis no plano atual) $0).

- **Break-even: never (each unit loses money) entrys a day.** Below that you lose money every month.
- **Profit margin at your plan** (100 a day): **-3%** of every sale, after every cost.
- Capacity: 300 a day.

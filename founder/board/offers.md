# Offers lens
Score: 3 | Vote: PASS

The lens fits a trading edge only loosely: the "buyer" is the founder, the "price" is the stake, and the "outcome" is the payout. It fits the panel-as-product fully, and I apply it there. For the edge itself I use one part of the lens, the likelihood of getting the outcome.

## What is strong
- Honest measurement. 1,853 fresh entries, a frozen baseline, a pre-declared 53% pass line and a 51% fail line. Few people in this market have proof discipline.
- Paper trading first. The cost of learning is zero.
- The panel (bankroll control, tax, projection, public bankroll link, RLS) is real, cheap-to-deliver stack material. It is the only part with a plausible product shape.

## What would kill it (the top 3, most dangerous first)
1. Likelihood of the outcome is near zero today. Observed 49.49% against 51.02% breakeven is 1.5 pt short, or 1.3 SE. That is about 10% likely if the true rate were 51%. Expected value is -$1.20 per entry (-$2,220.80 in total). The 53% target sits 3.5 pt, or about 2.9 SE, above the observation. The odds of reaching it are slim.
2. The strong-signal filter is not proof. 54.32% on 408 signals (SE ~2.5 pt) came from a cutoff picked after looking at the data. The clean sample is 16 entries at 50.0% (SE ~12.5 pt), which says nothing. Money management (Soros, stops) cannot make a negative-EV bet positive, and every variant runs on the same signals.
3. The panel as a product has a bad market. Binary-options retail traders feel the pain and are easy to reach, but they lose money, so they are weak payers. A tool that makes losing neater is a commodity with no guarantee, and the regulatory and ethical exposure is high. Without a verified edge, selling it sells a dream with no proof.

## The questions I would ask the founder
- If 30/11 shows 49-50%, do you stop, in writing, today?
- What is the stop rule for the filter, and how many clean entries do you need? About 1,000 clean ones give an SE of ~1.6 pt.
- Would you pay $X/month for the panel if your own edge were zero?
- Who besides you has used the panel for 30 days?

## If FUND IF: the conditions
Not FUND IF as it stands. Revisit only if:
- At least 800 clean entries after 29/10 reach 53% or more (lower bound above 51.02%).
- No further tuning of the filter happens during the test.
- Real money stays at zero until then.
- The panel is positioned as a bankroll-discipline tool with no promise of profit, and 5 outside traders use it before any price is set.
